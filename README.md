# Human Diet Institute

**Ambition: produce evidence strong enough to change dietary practice.** Discover which diets improve human health, for whom, why, and with what long-term tradeoffs.

Our starting hypothesis is that carnivore can deliver substantial benefits. Test it against credible alternatives and actively seek results that overturn it.

The central question: **does excluding plants add benefits beyond removing ultra-processed food, increasing protein, entering ketosis, or losing weight?**

## Start here

**Can people retain their digestive benefits while bringing particular plant foods back?** Develop a controlled reintroduction study among people already reporting improvement on carnivore. This could distinguish a useful targeted exclusion from a need to avoid all plants.

**Next deliverable:** a short, externally reviewed proposal defining one food comparison and the result that would change a dietary decision. See the [first study](EXPERIMENTS.md#first-project-from-question-to-test).

- [Evidence map](EVIDENCE.md): what existing studies establish and leave unresolved.
- [Research agenda](EXPERIMENTS.md): reintroduction, controlled feeding, and long-term outcomes.

## Research cycle

1. **Find the gap.** Check competing explanations and reproduce existing analyses where possible.
2. **Design the deciding test.** State what would support or overturn the hypothesis.
3. **Check feasibility.** Simulate the analysis and pilot delivery; confirm with a frozen plan and fresh data.
4. **Challenge the explanation.** Answer skeptical review with better comparisons and tests; preserve original results.
5. **Replicate and update.** Publish failures and harms; seek independent replication.

Inspired by Google Cloud AI Research's [ScientistTwo](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19644v1), demonstrated in computational research. This nutrition adaptation is our proposal. AI assists with evidence, analysis and criticism; human researchers own decisions and conclusions.

## Work in the open

Preregister using the [research record](RECORD.md). Publish protocols, code and permitted data; keep identifiable health data outside Git. Human studies require clinical oversight and ethics review. Feeling better and long-term health are separate claims.

**Scientific milestone:** one independently replicated finding that distinguishes competing explanations. **Status:** study development; no participants enrolled and no findings yet.
